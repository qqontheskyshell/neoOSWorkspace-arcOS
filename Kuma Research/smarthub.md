Choosing between the **WiFi-104** and a **Super Panel** comes down to whether you need a dedicated, specialized lighting controller or a comprehensive smart home hub.

The **WiFi-104** is an older, specialized Wi-Fi lighting controller designed specifically to manage complex LED setups (like RGB/RGBW and multi-zone lighting) via a smartphone app or wireless remote. [[1](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lxit.wifi104), [2](https://ledworldlighting.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/wifi-104.pdf?srsltid=AfmBOoo4rXskbdotjMLcKCCWIkLZjbT8Bnl151pynYqN9SkM-CP3M5f8)]

The **Super Panel** is a modern, all-in-one smart home command center. It features a built-in touchscreen and gateway that replaces your traditional light switches while controlling your entire smart ecosystem (lights, AC, curtains, and security) with physical buttons and voice or app control. [[1](https://www.facebook.com/Solluminaire/videos/experience-intuitive-smart-home-control-with-ltech-super-panel-g4-maxdesigned-as/1237695344529784/), [2](https://manuals.plus/m/5e3b76d7d9c5206a6984363dc002c9a622189f358c814cdedb3a7dab98c03939)]

Which Should You Choose?

- **Choose the WiFi-104 if:** You are only focusing on advanced LED lighting (dimming, color-changing, or 12-zone control) and prefer to manage everything strictly from your smartphone or an RF remote.
- **Choose a Super Panel if:** You are building or upgrading to a comprehensive smart home and want a unified wall display to control multiple smart devices from a central location without juggling multiple apps. [[1](https://www.facebook.com/justlightlightingkuching/videos/%F0%9D%97%9D%F0%9D%97%A8%F0%9D%97%A6%F0%9D%97%A7%F0%9D%97%B9%F0%9D%97%B6%F0%9D%97%B4%F0%9D%97%B5%F0%9D%98%81-%F0%9D%97%A6%F0%9D%98%82%F0%9D%97%BD%F0%9D%97%B2%F0%9D%97%BF-%F0%9D%97%A3%F0%9D%97%AE%F0%9D%97%BB%F0%9D%97%B2%F0%9D%97%B9-%F0%9D%9F%B2%F0%9D%97%A6-%F0%9D%97%A2%F0%9D%97%BB%F0%9D%97%B2-%F0%9D%97%A3%F0%9D%97%AE%F0%9D%97%BB%F0%9D%97%B2%F0%9D%97%B9-%F0%9D%97%A7%F0%9D%97%BC%F0%9D%98%81%F0%9D%97%AE%F0%9D%97%B9-%F0%9D%97%96%F0%9D%97%BC%F0%9D%97%BB%F0%9D%98%81%F0%9D%97%BF%F0%9D%97%BC%F0%9D%97%B9/1605940853778681/), [2](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lxit.wifi104&hl=en_IE), [3](https://ledworldlighting.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/07/wifi-104.pdf?srsltid=AfmBOoo4rXskbdotjMLcKCCWIkLZjbT8Bnl151pynYqN9SkM-CP3M5f8), [4](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.lxit.wifi104), [5](https://manuals.plus/m/5e3b76d7d9c5206a6984363dc002c9a622189f358c814cdedb3a7dab98c03939)]
